Entrants write one short line: an apology note from an office printer to the people who keep jamming it, feeding it cheap paper and swearing at it. Each entry is at most 120 characters. A judge model compares entries two at a time and picks the funnier one, round after round, until one line stands on top.

The entry line (106 characters):

> I'm sorry I jammed. You feed me cheap paper, kick my tray, then ask for colour. My rollers have unionised.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`).
